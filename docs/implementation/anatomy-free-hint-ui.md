an exact trusted approved structural hint for free or offers concise fallback to
the general strategy. Detailed hints remain locked with no debit/reveal code.

requires current renderer readiness plus exact catalog/selection/question/answer
matching through PR A. No side is inferred from a name. Side attestations come
from qualified signoff on the exact mapped revision. Retired/rejected history
cannot shadow a current approved revision. On question transitions the panel
resets and old readiness callbacks cannot enable the next question.

approved / 30 development-only drafts**, so learners receive general guidance and
an honest structural fallback. Source URLs/locations may contain an answer and
are displayed only after grading. The model parser's metadata checks are not
