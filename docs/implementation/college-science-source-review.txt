COLLEGE SCIENCE AUTHORING AND SOURCE REVIEW — 2026-10-04

Scope
The five established College titles and IDs are preserved. Each is an
introductory university module with a focused quantitative theme, not a claim
to replace an entire university survey course. Grade 1 is unchanged.

General Biology: enzyme kinetics, model assumptions, metabolic coupling.
General Chemistry: Gibbs energy, entropy, temperature and composition.
University Physics: harmonic dynamics, energy, damping and driving.
Earth Science: planetary radiation and carbon reservoir budgets.
Scientific Research: independent experimental units, design and inference.

Each module contains four measurable objectives, worked and guided examples,
assumption checks, subject-specific common mistakes, an evidence activity,
recap, 20 practice items in 7/7/6 tiers, three independent mastery items and
three locally authored figures. All assessment items are multiple choice;
no forced identification of multiword university terminology is introduced.

Sources consulted for factual verification
These are reference links, not runtime dependencies. Wording, numbers for
fictional exercises, questions and diagrams are original. No source images,
tables or third-party assets were copied. Existing licenses remain unchanged.

General Biology
- NIH/NCATS Assay Guidance Manual, Mechanism of Action Assays for Enzymes:
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK92001/
  Verified Michaelis-Menten interpretation and reversible competitive
  inhibition. The normal page returned a browser challenge on open; its
  indexed official text and printable search result were available.
- NIH/NCATS Assay Guidance Manual, Basics of Enzymatic Assays for HTS:
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK92007/
  Verified initial-rate conditions, substrate depletion and assay linearity.
- NCBI Bookshelf, Molecular Biology of the Cell, Protein Function:
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26911/
  Cross-check of catalysis and kinetic parameters. Km is explicitly NOT
  identified universally with substrate dissociation constant.

General Chemistry
- OpenStax/Rice University, Chemistry 2e, 16.4 Free Energy:
  https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/16-4-free-energy
  Verified Delta G = Delta H - T Delta S; Delta G = Delta G standard + RT ln Q;
  Delta G standard = -RT ln K. Exercises independently derive temperature
  crossover, inverse/squared K and activity exponents. Actual reaction Delta G
  is explicitly distinguished from its standard-state value. Constant Delta H
  and Delta S are stated approximations over the indicated temperature range.

University Physics
- OpenStax/Rice University, University Physics Volume 1, 15.1:
  https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-1-simple-harmonic-motion
- Same publisher and volume, 15.5 Damped Oscillations:
  https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-5-damped-oscillations
  Verified restoring force, displacement/velocity/acceleration relationships,
  mass/stiffness scaling and damping. Energy values are calculated independently
  from work and conservation. Position does not uniquely determine velocity
  sign. Finite damping limits real driven response; resonance is not described
  as universally infinite or as always peaking exactly at natural frequency.

Earth Science
- NASA Science, Climate and Earth's Energy Budget:
  https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/climate-and-earths-energy-budget/
- NOAA Ocean Service, What is the carbon cycle?:
  https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/carbon-cycle.html
- NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory, Basics of the Carbon Cycle:
  https://gml.noaa.gov/outreach/carbon_toolkit/basics.html
  Verified reflected versus absorbed radiation, infrared exchange, reservoirs
  and time-scale differences. Solar irradiance 1360 W/m2 and all flux examples
  are rounded illustrative model inputs, not current observational claims.
  Effective emission temperature is distinguished from surface temperature;
  molecule turnover is distinguished from removal of an excess CO2 inventory.

Scientific Research
- NIST/SEMATECH handbook, Randomized block designs:
  https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri332.htm
- NIST/SEMATECH handbook, Critical values and p values:
  https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section1/prc131.htm
- NIST/SEMATECH handbook, Confidence limits for the mean:
  https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda352.htm
- NIST/SEMATECH handbook, Relationship between test and confidence interval:
  https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section1/prc15.htm
  Verified blocking, prechosen significance, p-value interpretation and
  interval/test relationship. The multiplier 2 is always explicitly supplied
  as an approximation. Multiplicity arithmetic explicitly assumes independent
  tests. Random assignment is distinguished from random population sampling.

Second-pass calculations and scope audit
- Biology: 90*6/(2+6)=67.5; 120*9/(3+9)=90; inhibited 120*9/(9+9)=60.
  Two linked steps +12 and -30 total -18 kJ/mol only with a coupling mechanism.
- Chemistry: 30-250*0.100=5; crossover 300 K; 8.314*300*ln(0.1)/1000
  is approximately -5.743 kJ/mol. Reverse/double of K=4 gives 1/16.
- Physics: sqrt(200/0.5)=20 rad/s; T=0.31416 s; E=1 J. At A/2,
  U=0.25 J and speed=sqrt(3)=1.732 m/s. Fourfold mass doubles T.
- Earth: 1360/4=340; absorption 0.7*340=238. Albedo reduction 0.05
  adds 17 W/m2. Net 9-11 over four years removes 8 GtC; 3*44/12=11 GtCO2.
- Research: 6/sqrt(9)=2; 6/sqrt(36)=1; 4 +/- 2*1.5 gives [1,7].
  SE of two independent means with s=4 and n=16 is sqrt(2)=1.414.
  1-0.95^20=0.641514. Six pots with ten leaf readings remain n=6 per group.
