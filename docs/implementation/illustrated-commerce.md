# Illustrated commerce ? 0.15.1 Preview

Reward cards use offline native illustrations for shields, frames, science/streak badges and theme palettes. Category controls have matching icons. Reward previews open immediately in a scrollable dialog without changing the saved appearance. Wallet details can be expanded on demand; the account purchasing pause remains visible.

Purchase controls remain interactive when the learner cannot afford a reward: review shows exact cost, current balance and shortfall, with navigation to the real offline Subject World Map. This action awards no Credits. Funded guest purchases retain explicit confirmation, fresh-balance checks, durable writes and ownership guards. Pending purchases cannot be resubmitted. Account-backed purchases remain paused until the server price rollout is verified.

Free, Plus, Pro and Family plans have individual illustrated crests and prominent buttons opening a scrollable plan review. Free access remains available. Paid prices and benefits are proposed. Checkout and receipt verification are not configured, so these reviews never charge or activate a membership. A billing provider, approved final terms and verified receipt service are still required.

Validation covers confirmation/cancellation, changed balances, exact debits, inventory controls, insufficient-funds navigation, temporary try-on, plan review and 320px layouts with 160% text. Release checks require full analysis/tests, web output inspection, Android and Windows CI builds before promotion.
