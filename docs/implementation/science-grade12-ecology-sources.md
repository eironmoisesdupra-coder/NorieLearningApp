# Grade 12 Ecology authoring sources

Checked 2026-10-04. All prose, invented numerical examples and vector drawings are original. Sources informed scientific checks; no remotely hosted images or runtime network dependency were added.

- [USGS: Capture-recapture and removal methods for sampling closed populations](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/capture-recapture-and-removal-methods-sampling-closed-populations), White, Anderson, Burnham and Otis (1982). Closed-population estimation and its assumptions. The lesson limits MC/R to a simple two-sample model, identifies mixing, retained marks, catchability and closure, and does not treat it as exact or apply it when R = 0.
- [USGS: Capture-Mark-Recapture Science](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eesc/science/capture-mark-recapture-science). Observed counts and detection probability are distinct; changing survey conditions cannot automatically be interpreted as changing abundance.
- [Soberón and Arroyo-Peña (2017), PLOS ONE: Are fundamental niches larger than the realized?](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0175138). Primary research distinguishing fundamental conditions, realized occurrence and geographic limitations; notes that interactions need not always restrict a niche. The original moisture picture is explicitly a competition-only, one-dimensional hypothetical comparison, not measured species data or a universal containment rule.
- [National Park Service: Cycles and Processes](https://home.nps.gov/yell/learn/nature/cycles-and-processes.htm). Richness and abundance structure in biodiversity. The lesson explicitly defines its index as 1 − sum of squared sample proportions, avoiding ambiguity among Simpson conventions.

Independent arithmetic: quadrats 12/4 = 3 plants/m² and 3 × 100 = 300; stratified total 20 × 5 + 80 × 1 = 180; recapture 40 × 50/10 = 200 and 20 × 30/6 = 100. Diversity 4:4 gives 0.5; 7:1 gives 0.21875; 5:1 gives 1 − 26/36 = 0.277777… . No claims of index-derived ecosystem health or a universally safe harvest rate.

Progression: adds sampling design, area weighting, detection, closed-population inference, diversity metrics and niche tests beyond Grade 10 population change, resource limits, energy transfers and cascades. The sustainability section asks what evidence supports a decision rather than reteaching trophic production.
