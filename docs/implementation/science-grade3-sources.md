# Grade 3 Science authoring and factual review

The five Grade 3 lessons, 115 assessment items and 15 figures are original, locally authored content. The two new pictorial models are drawn with Flutter canvas primitives and use accessible Flutter text labels. Lessons and illustrations require no network connection. The links below record author-time checks; source pages are not runtime dependencies.

- **Plant Parts:** [Kew: Parts of the plant, teacher answer sheet](https://endeavour.kew.org/sites/endeavour/files/documents/2022-05/Kew-KS1-A%20seeds%20journey%20-%20discussion%20card%20answers.pdf) supports root anchoring/absorption, stem transport, leaves making food and flower/seed roles. The lesson distinguishes soil minerals from plant-made food, identifies a tree trunk as a stem, and identifies fruits by development from flowers and seeds rather than sweetness. The bean drawing shows branching roots beneath a soil cutaway, attached leaves, a flower and a pod with a seed cutaway. Pollination is introduced without claiming that every flower must produce a fruit.
- **Animal Adaptations:** [Natural History Museum: Convergent evolution](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/convergent-evolution.html) supports helpful inherited traits passing through survival and reproduction. [NPS: Yellowstone Survivor](https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/kidsyouth/get-wild-yellowstone-survivor.htm) supports structural versus behavioral adaptations and migration. [NPS: Snowshoe Hare, Olympic National Park](https://www.nps.gov/olym/learn/nature/snowshoe-hare.htm) documents populations that remain brown; the lesson therefore uses a qualified snowy-region seasonal-coat example. [NPS: Raptors](https://www.nps.gov/samo/learn/nature/birds_raptors.htm) supports talons gripping prey and hooked-beak feeding. Inherited adaptation is distinguished from an individual wish, training and temporary behavior. The paper-square activity models color contrast, not survival or an evolutionary experiment.
- **Matter & Changes:** [ACS: What is a Chemical Reaction?](https://www.acs.org/middleschoolchemistry/lessonplans/chapter6/lesson1.html) supports the new-substance distinction and state changes/dissolving as physical changes. [ACS: Coastal Chemistry](https://www.acs.org/education/activities/coastal-chemistry.html) supports iron reacting with oxygen to form rust and the role of water. Content rejects reversibility and a single color/bubble clue as definitions. Salt remains present when dissolved; only water leaves the salt-water dish by evaporation. The sealed-bag 20 g example explicitly requires that nothing enters or leaves. No child-operated heating, tasting, cleaners or sharp-metal handling is requested.
- **Force & Motion:** [NASA Glenn: Rocket Principles](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/rocket/TRCRocket/rocket_principles.html) supports unchanged rest or straight-line motion under balanced forces and speed/direction changes under unbalanced forces. The lesson includes a moving object under balanced forces and a force opposite current motion that first slows an object. The new force drawing uses equal 70-model-unit arrows on its balanced row, and 32 left versus 90 right on its unbalanced row. Length compares force strength; no measurement unit or travel-distance claim is made. Balanced vertical forces are explicitly omitted. The speed bars are distances of 20/40 cm measured over the same two seconds. The toy-car activity acknowledges variable hand pushes and asks for repeated trials.
- **Weather Patterns:** [NWS: Weather Instruments](https://www.weather.gov/media/meg/education/Weather_Instruments_handout1.pdf) supports instrument/property matching. [NWS: Wind Direction glossary](https://forecast.weather.gov/glossary.php?word=WIND+DIRECTION) supports the direction-from convention. [NWS: Weather Instrument Shelter](https://preview.weather.gov/rah/edu2) supports suitable shade for air-temperature measurements. [NOAA: The water cycle](https://www.noaa.gov/education/resource-collections/freshwater/water-cycle) supports evaporation, condensation, precipitation, runoff, soil pathways and many possible routes. The lesson distinguishes clouds containing droplets/ice from invisible vapor, measurement logs from processes, and observations from forecasts. Example rain totals are 2 + 6 + 0 + 8 = 16 mm, with Thursday’s 8 mm greatest. Equal 24-hour intervals and units are explicit. No daily rainfall guarantee is inferred from a cycle diagram.

Assessment review: each lesson contains seven foundation, seven intermediate and six application practice items, followed by three separate mastery items. All keys are taught in prose or explained figures; every item has four distinct options and feedback. Independent review caught the hare-population qualification and weak distractors; these were revised to use taught confusions rather than unrelated body changes. The Grade 2 melting-definition duplicate was replaced by an ice-cube scenario. Exact titles and same-grade prerequisite IDs remain stable, with the first prerequisite null.

Visual validation is provided in `test/science_grade3_picture_test.dart`: label/semantics checks at 244 px with 1.3× text, a pixel check of equal/unequal force extents, and an inspectable gallery at `build/science_grade3_picture_preview.png`. Root integration runs Flutter checks and inspects the generated output; the content author does not claim these tests passed before that run.
