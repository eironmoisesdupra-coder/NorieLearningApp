# Discovery Adventure — retired

The optional pixel-art investigation and subject-level Adventure Mode have been
removed from the app. Students now enter a subject, choose a grade, and proceed
directly to its lessons. The Discovery room, mission state/store, journal UI,
subject previews, and their runtime entry points are no longer part of the
product. Any old Discovery progress saved in device preferences is left
untouched but is no longer read by the app.

The published **v0.19.0+25** build predates this removal and may still include
the activity until a new build is published. This change does not remove or
alter the separately selectable Space Adventure audio theme.

Historical implementation and verification records remain in
[`docs/wip/2026-10-22-discovery-adventure`](../wip/2026-10-22-discovery-adventure)
and the Discovery section of [`NORIE_CURRENT_HANDOFF.md`](../NORIE_CURRENT_HANDOFF.md).
