# Grade 9 Plate Tectonics: source and authorship record

Reviewed 2026-10-04. Lesson prose, hypothetical quantitative data, assessments and Flutter vector models are original. No external images or runtime downloads are used.

- USGS, [Developing the theory](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/developing.html): convergence of seafloor age, magnetic reversals, spreading and earthquake/volcano distribution evidence.
- USGS, [Magnetic stripes and isotopic clocks](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/stripes.html): polarity records and independent dating.
- NOAA, [Age of the Seafloor](https://sos.noaa.gov/catalog/datasets/age-of-the-seafloor/): age patterns, age uncertainty, rates and asymmetry.
- NOAA, [What is a mid-ocean ridge?](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/mid-ocean-ridge/): new basaltic crust at spreading boundaries.
- USGS, [Understanding plate motions](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/understanding.html): relative rates, geologic averaging and geodetic measurements.
- USGS, [How fast do tectonic plates move?](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/how-fast-do-tectonic-plates-move): satellite measurements compared with ocean-floor magnetic records.
- USGS, [At what depth do earthquakes occur?](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-depth-do-earthquakes-occur-what-significance-depth): shallow interface and deeper slab earthquakes; depth affects source distance, not a unique magnitude.
- USGS, [What is an earthquake and what causes them to happen?](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-earthquake-and-what-causes-them-happen): frictional locking, slip and seismic energy.
- USGS, [Hotspots](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/hotspots.html): age-progressive volcanic chains and limitations of fixed-source assumptions.
- USGS, [Heat is deep and magma is shallow in a hot-spot system](https://www.usgs.gov/news/volcano-watch-heat-deep-and-magma-shallow-a-hot-spot-system): predominantly solid mantle and localized melting.

## Model boundaries

Magnetic stripes are an ideal mirrored qualitative section, not a dated reversal sequence. Dipping slab geometry is unscaled and numbered points identify source positions, not measured depth classes. GPS positions are invented one-dimensional eastward displacements relative to a consistent reference. The lesson explicitly distinguishes one-sided and full rates, conditional projections, and geologic versus modern averaging intervals. No forecast of individual earthquakes is made.

## Progression from Grade 6

Moves beyond naming boundary types into multiple independent observations, quantitative unit conversion, asymmetric rates, elastic rebound, depth rather than only epicenter maps, and uncertainty in hotspot/GPS interpretations. Twenty practice items follow 7 foundation / 7 intermediate / 6 application ordering, followed by three independent mastery items.
