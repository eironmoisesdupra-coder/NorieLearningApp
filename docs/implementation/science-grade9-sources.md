# Grade 9 Science: source and authorship notes

The first four lessons in `grade_9_science.dart` use original instructional prose, independently constructed assessment rows and locally authored diagrams. References were consulted on 2026-10-04 to verify scientific mechanisms and model boundaries. No external illustration, copied textbook exercise, remote lesson resource or licensed source asset is bundled. These links are documentation, not runtime dependencies.

The fifth lesson is documented separately in [Grade 9 Plate Tectonics sources](science-grade9-plate-sources.md).

## Biology Foundations

- OpenStax, Biology 2e, [Homeostasis](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/33-3-homeostasis): regulation within a range; negative feedback opposes the triggering departure; evaporation and temperature response.
- OpenStax, Biology 2e, [Passive Transport](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/5-2-passive-transport): diffusion, facilitated diffusion, dynamic equilibrium and osmosis.
- OpenStax, Biology 2e, [Active Transport](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/5-3-active-transport): transport against gradients coupled to energy; electrical gradients matter for ions.
- OpenStax, Biology 2e, [ATP](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/6-4-atp-adenosine-triphosphate): ATP turnover couples energy transfer to cellular work.
- OpenStax, Biology 2e, [Cellular Respiration introduction](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/7-introduction) and [Photosynthesis overview](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/8-1-overview-of-photosynthesis): complementary overall matter transformations, plant respiration and light input.

The equal-pressure osmosis model explicitly uses a water-permeable, solute-impermeable membrane and nonpenetrating solute. The pump example states a neutral solute and passive leak pathway. No health diagnosis or biological experimentation is requested. The temperature picture represents negative feedback; transport and energy figures are accessible comparison/process diagrams.

## Atomic Structure

- OpenStax, Chemistry 2e, [Atomic Structure and Symbolism](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/2-3-atomic-structure-and-symbolism): nucleus/electrons, atomic number, mass number, isotope notation, ion charge and weighted atomic mass.
- OpenStax, Biology 2e, [Atoms, Isotopes, Ions, and Molecules](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/2-1-atoms-isotopes-ions-and-molecules-the-building-blocks): isotope identity and biological chemistry context.

The isotope mixtures at 20/22 u, 30/32 u, and 50/55 u are invented calculation data, not measured natural abundances. Independently checked means: 20.5 u, 31.2 u and 54.0 u. Carbon-12/C-14 have 6p/6n and 6p/8n respectively; neutral forms each have six electrons. The picture uses schematic 2+4 shell populations and expressly does not represent exact trajectories or scale.

## Chemical Bonding

- OpenStax, Chemistry 2e, [Ionic Bonding](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-1-ionic-bonding): electrostatic attraction, extended lattices, simplest charge-balanced ratios and conduction through mobile ions.
- OpenStax, Chemistry 2e, [Covalent Bonding](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-2-covalent-bonding): shared pairs, bond polarity and energy required to break bonds.
- OpenStax, Chemistry: Atoms First 2e, [Ionic and Molecular Compounds](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-atoms-first-2e/pages/3-7-ionic-and-molecular-compounds): molecular versus ionic formula interpretation.

The NaCl lattice/H2 comparison is original code artwork. Charge checks explicitly cover MgCl2, Al2O3, CaO, K2S and transfer to supplied new ions. Covalent networks are acknowledged; the octet rule is bounded; partial charges are distinguished from full ionic charges. Molecular polarity is not assessed without geometry. Bond breaking requires energy; overall reaction energy includes bond formation.

## Motion & Forces

- OpenStax, Physics, [Speed and Velocity](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/2-2-speed-and-velocity): scalar/vector distinction and complete-trip averages.
- OpenStax, Physics, [Acceleration](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/3-1-acceleration): signed velocity changes, direction and acceleration.
- OpenStax, Physics, [Newton's Second Law](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/4-3-newtons-second-law-of-motion): net force, mass and acceleration.
- OpenStax, Physics, [Newton's Third Law](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/4-4-newtons-third-law-of-motion): equal/opposite interaction forces act on different objects.

Original worked cart diagram: 8 N right minus 2 N left = 6 N right; 2 kg gives 3 m/s² right. The guided missing-force example requires 10 N left against 4 N right to produce a 6 N left resultant. Mastery: (−3 − (−9))/3 = +2 m/s²; (17−7)/5 = 2 m/s² and a 3 s rest start gives 6 m/s right. Graph statements distinguish position–time from velocity–time slopes. Constant speed around a curve does not mean zero acceleration. Activities use paper records, not road or heavy-load experiments.
