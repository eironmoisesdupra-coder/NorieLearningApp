# Priority 2 — Lesson enrichment / educational quality

## Goal
Upgrade already-authored lessons and their quizzes rather than adding empty catalog breadth. Integrate with existing Curriculum Library, guided rehearsal and quality validator while preserving stable canonical lesson IDs, objectives, XP and mastery semantics.

## Instruction flow
Discover (hook) → Understand (concept and representation) → Watch (worked example) → Solve together (guided with hints) → Solve independently → Apply/transfer → Error analysis → Review and spaced retention. Provide return/review navigation, don't gate progress by scrolling alone.

## Grade adjustment
- G1–G3: compact child-friendly text, large accessible diagrams, Norie as optional visual guide, counting, matching, concrete manipulatives, gradually faded support. Avoid overwhelming animations and dense text.
- G4–G6: clear vocabulary, labeled diagrams, 2–4 step guided examples, short applied exercises.
- G7–G10: explicit prerequisites, multiple representations, worked solutions, qualitative vs quantitative application, common errors.
- G11–G12+: deeper derivations, units/significant figures, multi-step symbolic and numerical solutions, scientific limitations and interpretation.

## Math / Science mandatory teaching standard
For any quantitative lesson include concept and *why formula applies*, knowns and unknowns, applicable equation, dimensional units, legal transformations, step-by-step calculation, reasonableness check, equivalent answer policy and meaningful applied transfer question. Blend conceptual and solving assessment rather than all multiple-choice or all plug-and-chug. Avoid invented measurements.

## Rich question bank
Single-select, multiple-select, numeric + units with tolerances, fill-step, sorting/ordering, matching, diagram labeling and error diagnosis, implemented only where renderer/authoring is supported. Exact answer and explanation audited by domain. For image-dependent Anatomy/Science, illustrations must correspond to tested content and correct answer, not random illustrative stock art.

## Validation + editorial quality
- Every lesson gets objectives, rubric, sources, grade-band review, misconceptions and complete answer explanations.
- Subject-expert factual review for high-stakes or anatomical/medical content; model cannot independently claim qualified approval.
- Accessibility for small screens, contrast, diagrams alt text, font scaling and reduced motion; offline assets/license manifests.
- Content release is gated by published manifest, reviewed content revision and actual question bank verification.
- No replacement of legacy topic IDs or loss of existing student evidence. If objective changes substantively, use compatibility migration approved by editor.

## Pilot-first acceptance
Start with one representative G1 visual lesson, one G7–10 Math solving lesson and one G11–12 Science quantitative lesson; obtain reviewed screenshots/test results and exercise answer variants before scaling. Explicitly report number of authored/enriched lessons, not just code capability.
