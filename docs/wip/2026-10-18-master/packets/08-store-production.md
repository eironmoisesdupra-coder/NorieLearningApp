Currently signing workflow/device certification templates exist; do not mark signed/physically certified. Owner must configure protected keys/certs directly in GitHub, review signer fingerprints, run signed candidate pipeline. Physical validation needs five real device classes, offline app checks, Windows-teacher/Android-student Wi-Fi QR, reconnect and firewall/camera tests with verifiable evidence, signed artifact hashes, update migration backup/restore. Store readiness requires actual privacy policy, support, account deletion, legal identity, platform billing policies, age/parental purchase safeguards, screenshot/ratings/assets/licensing review, AAB/APK and Windows verification. No fake CI evidence, no auto-publication, no live payment without verified entitlements.
