# Curriculum basis and alignment status

## What the app currently says

The Math, Science, and English learning paths use authored subject lessons for
Grades 1-12 and College. They are focused learning paths, not complete yearly
curricula. Topic-by-topic alignment to official grade, quarter, and competency
codes has not been completed. Do not describe the current lessons as fully
DepEd- or CHED-aligned until that mapping is reviewed and tested.

The grade-level screen shows the verified rollout phase and calls out the
uncompleted mapping. The complete paragraph 8 of DO 010 includes Grade 2;
the earlier transcription that omitted it was incorrect.

## Implemented replacement paths

The published catalog now selects the replacement packs rather than generating
generic study-habit lessons from a list of planned titles. Displayed titles and
lesson counts come from the actual published lessons. References and coverage
limits are bundled with the app and can be opened without network access.

- Mathematics Grades 1-10: 98 units, including 40 semantically retained authored
  lessons and 58 newly authored units, with 784 practice and 294 mastery items.
- Science Grades 1-10: 80 units and 880 practice/mastery items. Grades 1-2 are
  integrated exploratory activities, not a claim that DepEd has a separate
  Science learning area at these grades.
- English Grades 1-10: 64 units, 704 questions, and 64 passage-linked models.
  Grade 1 is Language/first-language literacy support, not an invented official
  standalone Grade 1 English curriculum. Grades 4 and 7 use selected MATATAG
  references; other grades identify their legacy English references.
  Complete literature collections, sustained composition, and live speaking
  performance assessment remain outside this authored pack.
- Each replacement lesson includes objectives, an explanation, a labeled
  offline model, worked and guided examples, common mistakes, a recap, and
  lesson-specific practice and mastery items.

The reviewed MATATAG Mathematics guide covers Grades 1, 4, and 7, and the
reviewed Science guide covers Grades 4 and 7. Other grade packs use explicitly
identified legacy K to 12 references where current guides were not verified.
The rollout table below establishes which policy applies; it does not make
legacy-reference content equivalent to a verified current MATATAG mapping.

### Senior high school

Each subject has 12 lessons per grade: six core/application bridge lessons and
six separately labeled STEM or academic extensions. Grade 11 and Grade 12 have
distinct publication paths and prerequisite sequences. Retained extension
lessons preserve their topic/question IDs and existing XP rewards.

The bridge course names follow final DO 017, s. 2026. Broad content scope was
also checked against the official April 2025 consultation packet, which is a
**draft**, not a final competency guide. Draft schedules are not used to
override final transition policy. These paths do not claim to cover every
official core competency or all 26 courses in the final STEM cluster.

### Progress and validation boundaries

Previously earned XP and completion records remain in local storage. A retained,
equivalent lesson keeps its ID; an unrelated new outcome does not inherit old
completion credit. Historical authored packs remain available to regression
tests without being silently republished over the replacement paths.
Saved reading links and existing seasonal missions can still resolve historical
lessons. Active published lessons take priority when an equivalent ID is reused;
historical completions do not count as completion of unrelated new outcomes.

Tests check real publication, unique IDs, prerequisite validity, answer keys,
question explanations, offline model registration, readable phone rendering,
and preservation of existing progress. Passing these tests is not proof of
complete standards coverage; final official guides and a reviewed outcome map
remain necessary.

Across the three target subjects and 13 levels, the published replacement
catalog contains 332 lessons: 242 for Grades 1-10, 72 for senior high school,
and 18 for College, with 3,772 published practice/mastery entries. These counts
measure app content, not a percentage of official annual competencies covered.

## Verified DepEd rollout

DepEd Order No. 010, s. 2024 lists the following MATATAG implementation
phases:

| Grades | First school year in the listed phase |
|---|---|
| 1, 4, 7 | SY 2024-2025 |
| 2, 3, 5, 8 | SY 2025-2026 |
| 6, 9, 10 | SY 2026-2027 |

The source is paragraph 8 of the enclosure, printed page 2 (PDF page 4,
zero-based rendered page 03).

DO 017, s. 2026, paragraph 7 distinguishes the Strengthened Senior High
School transition: Grade 11 begins nationwide in SY 2026-2027; Grade 12
begins nationwide in SY 2027-2028. During SY 2026-2027, Grade 12 learners
outside pilot schools retain the 2016 K to 12 SHS curriculum. A general
announcement about nationwide SSHS adoption must not be interpreted as
both grades transitioning simultaneously.

DO 017 Table 1 names General Mathematics, General Science, and Effective
Communication and Mabisang Komunikasyon among the Grade 11 core subjects.
It aligns these with Mathematics in the Modern World, Science, Technology,
and Society, and Purposive Communication respectively. A named alignment
does not establish competency-by-competency coverage in this app.

## Guides reviewed

The locally available official DepEd MATATAG guides identify these scopes:

- Mathematics: Grades 1, 4, and 7.
- English: Grades 4 and 7.
- Science: Grades 4 and 7.
- Language: Grade 1.

The Mathematics guide was reviewed from a local PDF copy; its canonical
download URL still needs confirmation.

These are not a complete set of grade-level guides for the requested
Grades 1-12 scope. A Grade 1 Language guide should not be represented as a
separate Grade 1 English guide. The remaining subject/grade guides, quarterly
competencies, and English-language-area distinctions must be verified before
publishing detailed standards mappings.

## College general education

The college paths replace major-specific calculus, university physics, and
general biology with 18 original GE lessons across Mathematics in the
Modern World, Science, Technology and Society, and Purposive Communication.
The course titles are corroborated by DepEd DO 017, s. 2026, Table 1,
which explicitly cites CHED CMO No. 020, s. 2013.

Direct access to CHED's PDF was blocked, and public state-university
syllabi have not yet been verified. Consequently the original GE lessons
are not certified as exhaustive CHED-outcome coverage or as a reproduction
of a particular university syllabus. Their tests verify publication,
teaching structure, answer validity, and diagrams, not accreditation.

New lessons have new topic and question IDs. Earned XP and old completion
records are not deleted or transferred to unrelated GE outcomes.
