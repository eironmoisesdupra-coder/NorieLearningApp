**Full description draft:**

Learn with subject lessons, practice activities and study tools. NorieLearning
keeps core lessons and learning progress on your device so learning can continue
without an internet connection. Follow your progress, revisit lessons and use
Classroom sharing with a teacher on a supported local network.
